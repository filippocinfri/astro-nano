# Research papers per euro area NCB per year

This heatmap compares annual publication counts for euro area national central banks, using records indexed in the BIS Central Bank Research Hub.

<NcbPapersHeatmap data={dataByYear} />

## Interpreting the results

The heatmap shows the number of papers indexed for each institution and calendar year. Darker cells represent higher counts.

A cell with no entries should not automatically be interpreted as zero research output. Changes in the coverage of the BIS Research Hub can affect comparisons between institutions and over time.

## Methodology and limitations

The figures represent publications indexed in the BIS Central Bank Research Hub, not necessarily the complete research output of each national central bank.

Comparisons should account for differences in indexing coverage. The 2026 figures are year-to-date and should not be compared directly with full calendar years.
